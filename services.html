01

Government & Public Sector Advisory

Core Practice

Strategic and operational advisory for central, state, and local government bodies, public sector undertakings, and agencies. We work at the intersection of policy, administration, and technology — understanding that government mandates operate under constraints that private sector advisors frequently misread.

Our advisory covers the full policy-to-delivery spectrum: from helping bodies define what problem they are actually solving, to designing governance structures for major programs, to supporting procurement decisions with rigorous analysis. We understand India's regulatory frameworks, statutory timelines, financial delegation limits, and the political realities that determine which solutions are viable — not just technically correct.

We have worked with municipal corporations, development authorities, state departments, and central ministry programs. Our advisory is independent — we have no commercial arrangements with vendors or contractors that would bias our recommendations.

What we deliver

Program strategy and governance design
Stakeholder mapping and management plans
Policy analysis and regulatory assessment
Implementation roadmaps with KPIs
Vendor and contractor evaluation frameworks
Change management and transition planning

02

DPR, RFP & Bid Advisory

High Demand

A Detailed Project Report or Request for Proposal is only valuable if it accurately defines the problem, reflects real constraints, and is written to attract competitive bids from qualified firms. A poorly written tender attracts one bid — or none. A poorly structured DPR commits resources to solving the wrong problem.

We prepare DPRs, feasibility studies, RFPs, and tender documents from first principles. We begin with the problem definition — what outcome the procuring body actually needs — and work backward to the specification. This produces documents that attract competitive bids, provide evaluators with clear criteria, and withstand audit scrutiny from the Comptroller and Auditor General, vigilance bodies, and internal audit functions.

We also provide bid advisory: supporting the procurement process from pre-bid queries through bid evaluation, contract negotiation, and award documentation.

What we deliver

Detailed Project Reports (DPR)
Feasibility studies and cost-benefit analysis
Request for Proposals (RFP / RFQ / EOI)
Tender documents and SoW preparation
Bid evaluation frameworks and scoring matrices
Pre-bid query management and corrigendum support

03

Smart Cities & Urban Infrastructure

Specialisation

Integrated urban platforms for mobility, utilities, surveillance, public safety, environmental monitoring, and governance — designed with city operations teams, not around them. We have worked on Smart Cities Mission projects and understand the difference between technology deployed and technology actually used.

Our approach begins with city operations: who runs the system, who maintains it, what data they actually need to make decisions, and what institutional capacity exists. Technology architecture follows from operational requirements — not the reverse. This produces systems that city staff can operate independently after implementation, rather than systems that require continuous vendor support to function.

What we deliver

Integrated Command and Control Centre (ICCC) advisory
Urban mobility and traffic management systems
Public safety and surveillance architecture
Smart utilities — water, waste, energy monitoring
Urban data platforms and citizen services
Smart Cities Mission proposal preparation

04

AI, Analytics & Automation

Growing Practice

Applied AI, predictive analytics, and intelligent automation built to production — not proof-of-concepts that demonstrate potential and go no further. We test assumptions before committing resources: a PoC or pilot first, full build only when the evidence supports it and the operational integration pathway is clear.

For government clients, AI applications must be explainable, auditable, and compliant with data governance requirements. We design AI systems accordingly — not as black boxes, but as tools that decision-makers can interrogate and rely on. We have experience with computer vision, natural language processing, predictive maintenance, demand forecasting, and anomaly detection across government and enterprise contexts.

What we deliver

AI readiness assessment and strategy
Machine learning model development and deployment
Predictive analytics for operations and planning
Natural language processing for document automation
Process automation and workflow intelligence
AI governance and explainability frameworks

05

IT, Software & System Integration

Core Practice

Software development, ERP integration, digitisation, cybersecurity, and system integration for government and enterprise environments. Our measure of success is not delivery of a system — it is whether your team can operate it independently after we leave. Knowledge transfer is built into every engagement, not handed over as documentation at close.

Government IT environments carry specific constraints: legacy system dependencies, interoperability requirements with NIC and state IT infrastructure, data localisation obligations, and procurement rules governing software licensing. We design within these constraints from the outset, not around them.

What we deliver

Custom software development and delivery
ERP selection, implementation, and integration
Legacy system modernisation and data migration
Cybersecurity architecture and compliance
System integration and API design
Digital transformation roadmap and execution

06

GIS & Spatial Intelligence

Specialisation

Geospatial analytics, mapping platforms, and location intelligence for utilities, municipalities, infrastructure planning, land management, and natural resource governance. GIS is the foundational data layer for most urban and infrastructure programs — and most organisations significantly underuse the data they already have.

We design GIS platforms that integrate with operational systems — utilities management, asset tracking, planning approvals, field operations — so that spatial data becomes actionable, not just visual. Our work spans base map preparation, satellite and drone imagery integration, spatial analytics, and GIS-enabled decision dashboards.

What we deliver

GIS platform design and implementation
Spatial data acquisition and base map preparation
Satellite and drone imagery integration
Infrastructure and asset mapping
GIS-enabled planning and decision dashboards
Location analytics and spatial modelling

07

IoT & Cloud Infrastructure

Core Practice

Sensor networks, edge computing, cloud architecture, and real-time data platforms connecting physical infrastructure to enterprise decision systems. IoT deployments in government contexts require particular attention to data sovereignty, network resilience, device lifecycle management, and integration with existing operational technology — none of which can be retrofitted after deployment.

Our cloud architecture work covers multi-cloud environments across AWS, Azure, and GCP, with specific experience in GovCloud and NIC-compliant infrastructure requirements. We design for operational resilience — systems that continue to function under adverse conditions, not just under nominal load.

What we deliver

IoT sensor network design and deployment
Edge computing architecture
Cloud migration strategy and execution
Real-time data platform design
DevSecOps pipelines and infrastructure-as-code
Device management and IoT security

08

Training, Capacity Building & Manpower

Supporting Practice

Structured workshops, skill development programs, and staffing services for government bodies, institutions, and organisations undergoing digital and organisational transformation. Technology implementations fail at the adoption stage more often than the technical stage. Building the human capacity to use, maintain, and evolve systems is as important as the systems themselves.

Our capacity building programs are designed around the actual roles and workflows of the people who will operate systems — not generic training content. We also provide specialist manpower on project basis: domain experts, technical specialists, and program managers embedded in client teams for the duration of complex programs.

What we deliver

Role-specific technology training programs
Digital literacy and upskilling workshops
Program management capacity building
Specialist manpower and expert secondment
Train-the-trainer programs for sustainable delivery
Change management and adoption support
